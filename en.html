Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Developer Information

This application is developed and maintained by Liutalab / GreenPepper.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: green.pepper.lab@gmail.com

2. Data Collection

While all your calculations and generated documents are processed and stored locally on your device, we collect certain technical and purchase-related data to ensure the app functions correctly, to monitor stability, and to manage premium features or in-app purchases.

Specifically, we collect anonymous diagnostic data (such as crash logs and device information) and data strictly necessary to process payments and verify subscription status. We do not collect or transmit sensitive personal data, such as your name, email, or physical address, to our servers.

3. Local Storage

The app stores your preferences (such as theme and unit settings) locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data is strictly functional, remains on your device, and is never uploaded to any server.

The application allows users to generate and download documents, such as the user manual and summaries of the data entered. These files are created and stored locally on the user's device. The application does not automatically transmit these files to external servers. Users are solely responsible for the storage, management, and any further sharing of downloaded files.

4. Data Security

All data stored locally on your device (preferences, generated files) is protected by Android's built-in security mechanisms and sandboxing, and is accessible only to this application. Data collected by our integrated third-party services is transmitted securely using industry-standard encryption protocols.

5. Third-Party Services

This application uses integrated third-party services to process payments and improve app stability. These services may collect information used to identify your device or app installation. These services include:

  • RevenueCat: Used to process and manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. View Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Used to collect crash reports and diagnostic data to help us fix bugs and improve performance. View Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers.

Additionally, the application allows users to share data entered into the app, including generated summaries, through third-party applications installed on the device (such as WhatsApp). Data sharing is performed solely at the user's initiative. Once shared, the data is processed in accordance with the privacy policies of the selected third-party application.

6. Cookies

This application does not use cookies or any similar tracking technologies.

7. User Rights (GDPR – EU Users)

For data processed by third-party services (such as anonymous crash logs or purchase identifiers), users in the European Union are entitled to the rights provided under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectification, erasure, and portability.

Because we do not maintain a direct database of identified users on our own servers, any requests concerning data handled by Firebase or RevenueCat should be addressed according to their respective policies, though we are available to assist you in this process.

For any privacy-related request or concern, you may contact us at: green.pepper.lab@gmail.com

8. Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently received personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information from our records.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. Any changes will be reflected within the application's interface and on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
